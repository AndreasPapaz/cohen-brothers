How payment works

Payment schedule

Tuition is paid either yearly (in January only) or quarterly. Quarterly payments are due on the first of January, April, July, and October. A late fee applies if payment is missed. We do not accept monthly or per-class payments.

Payment methods

We accept:

  • Chase QuickPay / Zelle to cohensjudo@gmail.com
  • Venmo to @ACjudo8190
  • Cash or check

Credit cards are not accepted.

What's included

All prices are for full membership. You're welcome to attend 1, 2, or 3+ times per week — it's your choice. Tuition is not prorated for attending fewer days or for scheduled holidays. We recommend attending as often as your schedule allows to get the most from training.

Refunds & restarts

We have a no-refund policy. Planning ahead for tournaments, clinics, and visiting coaches requires commitments we make months in advance. If you stop training and later want to restart, you'll pay the current rates when you return.
